The primary mathematics curriculum in India, designed by NCERT under the National Curriculum Framework (NCF), follows a progressive, spiral approach. Topics introduced in early classes are revisited in later grades with higher abstraction and complexity.

The detailed breakdown of topics, subtopics, concepts, and skill levels for **Classes 1 through 5** is organized below:

---

## Class 1 Mathematics

*Focus: Concrete learning, spatial understanding, single-digit and two-digit numbers up to 99, and basic sensory estimation.*

| Topic | Subtopics | Key Concepts Covered | Conceptual & Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shapes & Spatial Understanding** | Inside-Outside, Bigger-Smaller, Top-Bottom, Near-Far | Spatial vocabulary, 2D shape identification (Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle), rolling vs. sliding objects. | **Concrete & Observational:** Focuses on tactile identification, visual sorting, and relative spatial positioning without formal definitions. |
| **Numbers 1 to 9** | Counting, Grouping, Order of Numbers | Number-object matching, counting up to 9, missing numbers, concept of zero ($0$). | **Foundational:** Building number sense, ONE-to-ONE correspondence, and basic quantity recognition. |
| **Addition & Subtraction (1 to 9)** | Combining Collections, Taking Away | Picture-based addition/subtraction, adding zero, horizontal addition ($3 + 2 = 5$). | **Pictorial Arithmetic:** Solving addition and subtraction problems using visual counters and physical objects. |
| **Numbers 10 to 20** | Tens & Ones, Ordering Numbers 10–20 | Making bundles of 10, place value concepts (1 Ten + 4 Ones = 14), sequential counting. | **Place Value Introduction:** Transitioning from single units to bundled groups of ten. |
| **Patterns** | Visual Patterns, Number Sequence Patterns | Repeating shape sequences (A-B-A-B), simple ascending number patterns. | **Logical Reasoning:** Pattern recognition through physical visual attributes. |
| **Measurement** | Length & Weight Comparison | Non-standard units (handspan, fingers, pace), terms: Longer/Shorter, Taller/Shorter, Heavier/Lighter. | **Sensory Estimation:** Direct visual and tactile comparisons without standard metric units. |
| **Data Handling & Money** | Collecting Data, Indian Currency | Counting repeated items in pictures, identifying coins (₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10) and basic currency notes. | **Practical Familiarity:** Everyday real-life identification of objects and money. |

---

## Class 2 Mathematics

*Focus: Extension of numbers up to 100, introductory mental math, basic 3D shapes, and structured grouping.*

| Topic | Subtopics | Key Concepts Covered | Conceptual & Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shapes & Spatial Geometry** | 2D/3D Shapes, Straight & Curved Lines | Edges, corners, tracing flat faces of 3D objects (cubes, cylinders), line orientation (standing, slanting, sleeping). | **Tactile & Structural:** Connecting 3D physical objects to 2D traces; recognizing line orientations. |
| **Numbers up to 100** | Counting in Groups, Tens and Ones, Expanded Form | Skip counting by 2s, 5s, and 10s, building numbers up to 99 using Tens and Ones, comparing numbers ($>$, $<$, $=$). | **Structural Number Sense:** Grouping efficiency and early place-value notation. |
| **Addition & Subtraction (Up to 99)** | 2-Digit Addition/Subtraction, Word Problems | Addition without regrouping, breaking numbers for addition ($23 + 12 = 20 + 10 + 3 + 2$), basic word problems. | **Algorithmic Mechanics:** Transitioning from counting on fingers to split-number mental addition. |
| **Measurement (Length, Weight, Capacity)** | Non-Standard & Standard Intro | Comparing capacities (buckets vs. cups), estimating heavy vs. light, measuring using footsteps/handspans. | **Comparative Measurement:** Developing an understanding of volume and weight capacities through container comparisons. |
| **Time & Calendar** | Days of the Week, Months, Seasons | Sequence of events (morning, afternoon, night), reading simple calendar dates and sequence of months. | **Chronological Orientation:** Understanding time cycles, daily routines, and calendar organization. |
| **Data Handling & Patterns** | Block Diagrams, Pattern Rules | Recording data using tally marks/pictorial symbols, extended number patterns ($2, 4, 6, 8...$). | **Representational Organization:** Organizing collections into simple structured lists or picture charts. |

---

## Class 3 Mathematics

*Focus: 3-digit numbers (up to 1000), introduction to formal multiplication/division, standard units of measurement, and 2D symmetry.*

| Topic | Subtopics | Key Concepts Covered | Conceptual & Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Numbers up to 1000** | Place Value, Number Names, Expanded Form | 3-digit place values (Hundreds, Tens, Ones), skip counting by 50s and 100s, forming largest/smallest 3-digit numbers. | **Extended Number Literacy:** Transitioning to 3-digit abstraction and standard place-value charts. |
| **Addition & Subtraction with Regrouping** | Carrying Over, Borrowing, Estimation | 3-digit addition and subtraction with carry/borrow, mental math strategies, checking subtraction using addition. | **Operational Competency:** Mastering multi-digit columnar operations with regrouping. |
| **Multiplication (Basic)** | Repeated Addition, Multiplication Tables | Concept of equal groups, tables from 1 to 10, multiplying 2-digit numbers by 1-digit numbers, lattice/box method. | **Conceptual Foundation:** Linking repeated addition directly to multiplication tables and algorithms. |
| **Division (Introduction)** | Equal Sharing, Equal Grouping | Division as equal distribution, relation between multiplication and division ($3 \times 4 = 12 \implies 12 \div 4 = 3$). | **Structural Intuition:** Intuitive understanding of division as reverse multiplication and equal sharing. |
| **Shapes, 2D Nets & Symmetry** | Tiling, Symmetrical Patterns, 2D Views | Mirror halves, line symmetry, tiling patterns (tessellations), top/front/side views of simple objects. | **Visual-Spatial Geometry:** Analyzing symmetry, spatial perception, and floor patterns. |
| **Measurement (Standard Units)** | Meters/Centimeters, Grams/Kilograms, Liters/Milliliters | Standard units of length ($\text{m}, \text{cm}$), weight ($\text{kg}, \text{g}$), and capacity ($\text{L}, \text{mL}$); reading weighing scales and rulers. | **Standardized Measurement:** Moving from non-standard measurements to standard SI metric units. |
| **Time & Money** | Clock Reading, Currency Operations | Reading time in hours and minutes (e.g., half-past, quarter-to), converting rupees to paise, making simple bills. | **Applied Life Skills:** Reading analog clocks and computing basic money transactions. |

---

## Class 4 Mathematics

*Focus: 4-digit and 5-digit numbers, formal multi-digit multiplication and long division, fractions, perimeter, and perimeter/area concepts.*

| Topic | Subtopics | Key Concepts Covered | Conceptual & Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Numbers up to 100,000** | 4-Digit & 5-Digit Numbers, Indian Place Value | Thousands and Ten-Thousands place, building and comparing 5-digit numbers, estimation and rounding off. | **Advanced Number Systems:** Working comfortably with large numerical values and formal place-value systems. |
| **Multiplication & Division** | Long Division, Multi-Digit Operations | Multiplying 2-digit by 2-digit numbers, division with remainders, word problems involving multi-step arithmetic operations. | **Algorithm Mastery:** Performing complete long division algorithms ($\text{Dividend} = \text{Divisor} \times \text{Quotient} + \text{Remainder}$). |
| **Fractions** | Unit Fractions, Proper Fractions, Equivalent Intro | Parts of a whole ($\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{3}{4}$), numerator and denominator, visual fraction representations, shaded regions. | **Proportional Thinking:** Understanding non-integer quantities and visual fractional parts. |
| **Geometry & Circles** | Circles, Nets of 3D Objects, Angle Intro | Radius, diameter, center of a circle; drawing circles using a compass; 2D nets of cubes and open boxes. | **Constructive Geometry:** Basic geometric constructions using tools like compasses and rulers. |
| **Perimeter & Area** | Boundary Length, Tile Counting | Perimeter of simple 2D shapes (rectangles, squares), counting grid squares to estimate surface area. | **Spatial Quantification:** Distinguishing between boundary distance (perimeter) and flat space covered (area). |
| **Metric Conversions & Time** | Standard Unit Conversions, 24-Hour Clock | Converting $\text{km}$ to $\text{m}$, $\text{kg}$ to $\text{g}$, $\text{L}$ to $\text{mL}$; 12-hour vs 24-hour clock formats; elapsed time calculation. | **Mathematical Conversions:** Applying rate-based unit conversions and timeline math. |
| **Data Handling** | Pictograms, Bar Charts Intro | Reading and interpreting bar charts, pictorial representation with key scales (e.g., 1 icon = 10 items). | **Analytical Representation:** Transitioning to scaled data charts and quantitative tables. |

---

## Class 5 Mathematics

*Focus: Large numbers (Lakhs and Crores), operations with fractions and decimals, formal area/volume formulas, angles, and rotational symmetry.*

| Topic | Subtopics | Key Concepts Covered | Conceptual & Skill Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Large Numbers & Operations** | Indian & International Systems, Multi-step Problems | Numbers up to 7-8 digits (Lakhs, Crores), place value comparisons, real-world context applications (speed, distance, commerce). | **High-Level Arithmetic:** Solving complex multi-step real-world problems with large values. |
| **Shapes & Angles** | Angles (Right, Acute, Obtuse), Degree Clock | Identifying angle types in daily objects, using a protractor, understanding $90^\circ, 480^\circ, 180^\circ$ turns. | **Formal Geometry:** Measuring and categorizing angles quantitatively using standard units. |
| **Symmetry & Rotations** | Rotational Symmetry, Turns ($\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}$) | Half-turns, quarter-turns, patterns that look the same after rotation, line of symmetry extension. | **Transformation Geometry:** Understanding rotational invariance and geometric transformations. |
| **Factors & Multiples** | LCM, HCF, Prime/Composite Numbers | Common factors, common multiples, factor trees, prime factorization, divisibility rules. | **Number Theory:** Exploring structural properties of numbers, divisibility, and prime factors. |
| **Fractions & Decimals** | Operations on Fractions, Intro to Decimals | Addition/subtraction of like/unlike fractions, fraction-to-decimal conversion ($\frac{1}{10} = 0.1$, $\frac{1}{100} = 0.01$), tenths and hundredths place. | **Rational Number Operations:** Conceptualizing fractional parts as decimal place values. |
| **Area, Perimeter & Volume** | Standard Formulas, 3D Cuboids | Area of rectangle ($\text{L} \times \text{B}$) and square ($\text{side}^2$), intro to Volume (counting $1\text{ cm}^3$ cubes, $\text{L} \times \text{B} \times \text{H}$). | **Dimensional Quantifications:** Calculating 2D surface areas and 3D volumetric space using algebraic formulas. |
| **Data & Mapping Skills** | Maps, Scales, Tally Marks Frequency | Reading scale maps (e.g., $1\text{ cm} = 10\text{ km}$), drawing simple floor plans, tally mark frequency tables. | **Spatial Mapping & Statistics:** Interpreting structural spatial blueprints and statistical frequency. |
